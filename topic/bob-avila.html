Bob Avila — A Name Synonymous With Reined Cow Horse

Bob Avila is one of the most recognized names in reined cow horse and western horsemanship. His two World's Greatest Horseman titles — won in 2000 and 2007, seven years apart — demonstrate an extraordinary longevity at the top of the sport. Avila's first WGH title came in just the second year of the competition's existence, establishing him immediately as one of the sport's elite.

His 2007 championship, won years after his first, showed that Avila's ability to develop and present champion-caliber horses was not a one-time achievement but a sustained hallmark of one of reined cow horse's premier training programs. He is respected not only for his competitive record but for his contributions to the education of riders and the advancement of western horsemanship across decades.

Avila's two WGH titles, won seven years apart, make him one of just a handful of riders to win the championship in multiple eras — a testament to consistent excellence at the sport's highest level.
