Ron Ralls — Champion and Father of a Champion

Ron Ralls is one of the World's Greatest Horseman's most storied figures — not only as a two-time champion in his own right, but as the father of 2023 WGH champion Phillip Ralls. The Ralls family is the only father-son pairing in the event's history to each claim the title, a dynasty that spans two decades of reined cow horse competition and speaks to a horsemanship tradition passed from one generation to the next.

Ron's back-to-back titles in 2003 and 2004 placed him among the sport's elite during a highly competitive era of WGH history. His ability to win consecutive championships — performing at the highest level across three demanding phases two years in a row — demonstrated a mastery of the complete reined cow horse skill set that defined his legacy as a trainer and competitor.

The Ralls family is the only father-son duo in World's Greatest Horseman history — Ron won in 2003 and 2004, and his son Phillip claimed the title in 2023, cementing a family legacy unique in the sport.

The Ralls Dynasty

When Phillip Ralls claimed the 2023 WGH title, he completed one of the most remarkable family stories in the sport's history. Ron had laid the groundwork — winning back-to-back titles and establishing the Ralls name as synonymous with WGH excellence. Phillip grew up steeped in that tradition, trained in an environment built around the skills and values required to compete at the WGH level. His 2023 championship fulfilled a family legacy two decades in the making.
