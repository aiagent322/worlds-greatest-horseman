John Swales — Two-Time World's Greatest Horseman

John Swales joins an elite group of riders who have won the NRCHA World's Greatest Horseman more than once. His titles in 2017 and 2020 — three years apart — demonstrate the consistency at the top of the sport that defines the WGH's greatest competitors. Winning in different years, with different horses, against fields of elite competitors, requires a training program and competitive preparation that goes beyond talent alone.

Swales' 2020 championship is particularly notable given the disruptions to competition schedules during that period, making the achievement even more meaningful as a statement of where Swales stood in the sport's competitive hierarchy at that time.

Two WGH titles across 2017 and 2020 place John Swales among a very small group of riders who have proven their championship caliber across multiple years of elite competition.
